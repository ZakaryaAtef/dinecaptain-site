DineCaptain

Restaurant and café management: the POS, tables, kitchen, menu, recipes and delivery.

DineCaptain runs a restaurant, a café or a quick-service counter from Windows: dine-in, takeaway and delivery orders, the tables and the waiters, the kitchen display, the menu with its sizes, options and meals, recipes and ingredient stock, the call center, reservations and the restaurant's reports. It is made for restaurants and cafés in Egypt and the region, and works in Arabic, English, French, German, Spanish, Italian and Turkish.

Get it from Microsoft Store

What it does

  • A POS for dine-in, takeaway, pickup and delivery: rounds sent to the kitchen, voids with a manager's PIN, discounts with reasons, the service charge and VAT, pre-bills, split bills, cash, card, wallet and tips.
  • A floor plan and a live table map; move, merge and transfer orders between tables; a captain screen for waiters; reservations and a waitlist.
  • Kitchen stations, a kitchen display with timers, and kitchen tickets to network (ESC/POS) printers through the print agent.
  • A menu with sizes, options, extras, meals, dayparts and price lists, sold-out items, and a QR menu for guests' phones.
  • Recipes that take ingredients out of stock when the bill is paid, purchases, waste, stock counts, food cost and menu engineering.
  • A call center with customers' saved addresses, delivery zones, a delivery board and drivers' cash.
  • Restaurant reports, cashier shifts with X and Z reports, branches, staff permissions, an audit log, and backup and restore.
  • Counter and takeaway sales keep working when the internet drops.
  • Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA) e-receipts, with a practice mode.

How it works

DineCaptain is the Windows app of an online service, so it needs an account and an internet connection. Open the app, choose Create a new restaurant on the sign-in screen, then add your staff in Users & permissions. Every PC, tablet and phone of the restaurant signs in to the same restaurant, so the POS, the kitchen and the office see the same orders, menu and stock. Staff can also use DineCaptain in a web browser at dinecaptain.marlysoft.com. DineCaptain is currently free.
